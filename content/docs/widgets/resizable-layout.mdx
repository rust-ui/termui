---
title: Resizable Layout
description: Two horizontal areas sized by a caller-controlled percentage.
---

`ResizableLayout` computes two side-by-side areas from a percentage. Your application owns resize input and updates the ratio.

<RustDemo name="resizable-layout" title="Resizable Layout" rows={7} code={`use termui_widgets::resizable_layout::ResizableLayout;

let [sidebar, main] = ResizableLayout::new(30).areas(frame.area());`} />
