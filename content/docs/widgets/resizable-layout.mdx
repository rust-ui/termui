---
title: Resizable Layout
description: Two horizontal areas sized by a caller-controlled percentage.
---

`ResizableLayout` computes two side-by-side areas from a percentage. Drag the divider, or press Tab to activate it and use the arrow keys. The active state and panel widths update as you resize.
