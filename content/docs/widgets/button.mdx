`Button` supports shadcn-inspired `Default`, `Secondary`, `Destructive`, `Outline`, `Ghost`, and `Link` variants. Your app owns focus, keyboard input, and activation.

## Interactive demo

This live Ratatui app runs in Rust through Ratzilla and WebAssembly. It renders all six button variants. Click **Increment +1** or press Space to update the counter; click **Reset** or press R to clear it.

## Usage

```rust
use termui_widgets::button::{Button, ButtonVariant};

Button::new("Save")
    .variant(ButtonVariant::Default)
    .focused(true)
    .render(frame, area);
```
