---
title: Panel
description: Rounded panel shell for Ratatui widgets, with optional top and bottom titles.
---

`Panel` builds a rounded Ratatui `Block`. Add optional titles and border styling, then attach it to any widget with `.block()`.

<RustDemo name="panel" title="Panel" rows={13} code={`use ratatui::{
    style::{Color, Style},
    widgets::Paragraph,
};
use termui_widgets::panel::Panel;

frame.render_widget(
    Paragraph::new("Ready").block(
        Panel::new()
            .title("Activity")
            .bottom_title("Esc Back")
            .border_style(Style::default().fg(Color::Cyan))
            .block(),
    ),
    area,
);`} />
