`ConfirmationPrompt` lays out a question and two action labels. Your application handles the choices.

<RustDemo name="confirmation-prompt" title="Confirmation Prompt" rows={5} code={`use termui_widgets::confirmation_prompt::ConfirmationPrompt;

let prompt = ConfirmationPrompt::new("Delete this file?")
    .actions("Cancel", "Delete")
    .line();`} />
