## 21 September 2026 - Interactive Ratatui Widget Demos

We added keyboard-driven demos for 33 Ratatui widgets, configurable Button and Card shapes, and one shared page for the separate Rust and WebAssembly demos.

Follow [Term/UI on GitHub](https://github.com/rust-ui/termui) for source history and future releases.
